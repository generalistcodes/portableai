PortableAI

Your own AI, fully local

Chat from your browser or your phone, powered by models you control. No cloud, no accounts, nothing leaves your network.

View on GitHub See what it does

Named personas

Build a persona from a base Ollama model plus a system prompt and parameters — a terse mentor, a patient explainer, whatever you need.

Private by default

Runs on your own machine against your own Ollama install. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you explicitly turn on an update check.

Saved, searchable chats

Every conversation is titled automatically, archivable, and searchable by title or message content — not lost when you close the tab.

iPhone companion

Pair your phone once with a PIN shown on your laptop, then chat with your personas from anywhere on your home network.
